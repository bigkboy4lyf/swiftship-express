Bank transfer is offered as a payment option on every invoice, alongside card checkout. The parent account is the fallback bank transfer account used for any destination that doesn't have its own account set below -- set it once and it covers every country by default.

Demurrage & Storage Fees

These rates apply uniformly to every shipment platform-wide -- setting a rate does not charge anyone by itself. To actually start charging a specific shipment, open it from All Shipments and turn on demurrage or storage from that shipment's detail view; the customer is notified immediately when you do. From then on, it's charged the rate below once every 24 hours, with the customer notified of their new balance each time. Charging stops automatically once a shipment is paid in full or delivered.
