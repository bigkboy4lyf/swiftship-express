Quick Help

Find answers to common questions

How do I get a shipping quote?

Go to Get Quote in the sidebar, enter your origin, destination and package details, and we'll calculate an instant estimate.

How do I track a shipment?

Use Track Package in the sidebar or the tracking box on your dashboard, and enter your tracking number.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept card checkout and bank transfer. Both options are shown when you pay an invoice.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times depend on the service type you choose, from express (1-3 days) to economy (10-20 days).

How do I update my saved addresses?

Go to Addresses in the sidebar to add, edit, or remove saved addresses on your account.
